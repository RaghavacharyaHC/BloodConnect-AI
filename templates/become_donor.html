Your Digital Donor Pass

Every registration creates a secure, verifiable record in our local community database. Here is how your donor pass works:

  • 🪪 Digital Donor ID Card: Generates a unique Donor ID (e.g., BC-2026-XXXX) showing your blood group, city, and a dedicated digital badge.
  • 📲 Unpredictable QR Profile Token: The generated QR code points only to your public verification token URL. No personal contact information or email is ever encoded inside the QR image.
  • 🛡️ Contact Privacy Masking: Your phone number is masked (e.g. +91 98*****210) on all public screens to protect you against spam and unsolicited calls.
  • 💉 Genuine Donation History: Donation records cannot be self-submitted or fabricated. They can only be recorded and stamped verified by authorized hospital blood bank staff.

Medical Eligibility Criteria

Volunteers must be between 18 and 65 years old, weigh at least 45 kg, and be in general good health.
