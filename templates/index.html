How BloodConnect Works

An organized 3-step workflow designed to save time when every second counts.

Step 1

Search or Request

Enter the needed blood group, city or college location to find immediately available units or broadcast an emergency distress call.

Step 2

Match Compatible Donors

The platform filters matching donors based on medical compatibility, current availability, and proximity, protecting personal data.

Step 3

Donate & Verify with QR

Donors present their Digital ID card. Hospital staff scan the QR code to log and verify the donation, keeping records 100% genuine.
